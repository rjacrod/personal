About

I am a founder and researcher working at the intersection of ML and optimization, especially with spatial, graph-based, and mobility applications.

I currently run Modal, a multimodal microtransit software startup I cofounded with Pascal Van Hentenryck. Before that, I researched under Pascal at a National AI Institute before leaving my ML PhD at Georgia Tech to start Modal.

On the side, I prototype small projects with people I'd like to build with long-term. If you're technical and working on or thinking about something interesting, I'd love to hear from you.
